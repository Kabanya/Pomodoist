# Pomodoist 1.1.4

Habit history, daily rhythms, and project archiving.

Stable tag · Source date: 2026-10-04 · Commit: [`5a71c915`](https://github.com/Kabanya/Pomodoist/commit/5a71c915f0e70c581252095c83f54206e158765a)

Changes compared with `v1.1.3`.

## User experience changes

- Review habit history and organize habits by morning, afternoon, evening, or night, including targets for separate periods of a day.
- Switch between habit list and rhythm views.
- Archive projects to remove them from active planning without deleting their contents.
- Choose an emoji avatar in account settings and revoke account sessions.
- Use improved bottom navigation and task selection; task-list updates do less unnecessary work.

## Backend and developer changes

- Backend and agents: MCP/OpenClaw gain habit tools, including habit management and check-ins. Period targets and check-in periods extend habit payloads.
- Database compatibility: local schema version 10 adds check-in day periods; legacy marks receive deterministic period assignments. Habit and project-file migrations are recorded.
- Account security: session revocation and account request handling are extended.
- Storage operations: cleanup workers retain compatibility with earlier contracts; worker prerequisites and database-only quota fixtures are documented.
- Performance and validation: task-list update work is reduced; performance reports and migration assertions are made portable. Windows registers the emoji plugin.
