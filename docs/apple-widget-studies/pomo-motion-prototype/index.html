A motion sketch, not a native widget

This prototype moves existing Pomo stills and switches expressions. It does not play skeletal 3D animation, animate facial features, or prove WidgetKit playback. The mock controls are outside the widgets and change sample data only. View the placement studies.

One event. One finite reaction.

Every motion lasts exactly two seconds and settles into a still pose. A new event cancels the previous reaction. Changing appearance never replays motion. System Reduce Motion and the preview checkbox stop movement immediately; the new expression and data still appear. WidgetKit timing and supported transitions require a separate native check.
