### Cover restoration

the approved individual compositions with one generic layout, removed Pomo and
illustrations, substituted navigation labels for marketing headlines and changed
the series order/meaning. Pixel repeatability did not validate those decisions.
The owner supplied the original Mac contact sheet as the reference. The
restored covers replaced the partial draft uploads. The live 1.1.7 listing was
unchanged.

artwork, marketing messages and order. Changes are limited to current app
captures and translated cover copy. The screenshot harness now registers the
double-qualified Geist families used by the Shadcn calendar; the missing test
font produced the reported white rectangular glyphs. This capture defect does
not by itself establish a defect in the signed build. Search palettes are
cropped using their actual current bounds. Legacy voice illustrations/insets
and the Mac menu-bar inset are retained in English.

The restored 200 iOS and 100 Mac covers passed two independent captures and
renders with identical hashes and provenance. All current capture/renderer
source hashes still match their manifests. Automated text measurements,
the seven Apple tooling tests, targeted Dart analysis, the full 330-image PNG
matrix check and OCR of 20 new Mac captures passed. OCR checked all ten UI
languages and readable calendar dates. The separate native Watch renderer also
passed its 30-image rendering check.

On November 7, App Store Connect reported all 330 images complete across 40
sets in ten locales. Read-back confirmed the display order and source checksums
for the 300 replacement covers and 30 retained Watch screenshots, including

### Initial transfer checkpoint (historical)

The initial remote transfer was interrupted. The asc uploader's checksum
polling, compounded by repeated status reads, exhausted Apple's hourly API
request quota. Apple returned HTTP 429 with a reset around 21:00 UTC on
November 6. All owned transfer and polling processes were stopped; temporary
private-key copies were removed. Do not resubmit the original broad `--replace`
commands: some locales are already complete and others are partially uploaded.

The receipts and resume snapshots are retained under the build-133 output
directory. The authenticated website remains available. Its file-chooser
automation requires the ChatGPT Chrome extension's local-file permission; the
owner was asked whether to grant that permission or wait for the API reset.
No extension security setting was changed by the preparation tooling.

At this checkpoint, the two unsent App Review drafts were still empty. The
final delivery and review draft state are recorded in Cover restoration above.
