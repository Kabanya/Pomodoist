Pomodoist brings a task planner, habit tracker, and Pomodoro timer together. Organize tasks into projects, see what is due today, and review the time you spend in focus sessions.

Tasks, habits, and the timer work locally without an account. Sign in to a Pomodoist account to sync supported data across devices or collaborate in shared projects.

Account features connect to the hosted Pomodoist service and Supabase. Account verification opens Cloudflare Turnstile in your browser. Google and Apple sign-in and Google Calendar and Outlook integrations use those services when you choose to connect them.

This edition has its own Android app ID. It can be installed alongside other Pomodoist editions. Local data is stored separately.
